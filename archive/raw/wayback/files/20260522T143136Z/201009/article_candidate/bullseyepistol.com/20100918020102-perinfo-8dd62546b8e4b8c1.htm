Where is Camp Perry?

U.S. Army National Guard Camp Perry is located in North Ohio not far from the beaches of Sandusky Bay of Lake Erie just west of Port Clinton. As you may have guessed, there are plenty of activities and attractions to keep you and your family busy when you are not on the range. While your competitor packet from the NRA will include a map of the entire area, use the resources below to plan your vacation.
