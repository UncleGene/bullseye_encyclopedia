Where is Camp Perry?

U.S. Army National Guard Camp Perry is located in North Ohio not far from the beaches of Sandusky Bay of Lake Erie just west of Port Clinton. As you may have guessed, there are plenty of activities and attractions to keep you and your family busy when you are not on the range. In addition to the map and other information included in your NRA competitor packet, the resources below can help you plan your important week.
