THE TRACK RECORD OF TOURNAMENT

Match Directors and Stat Officers who are using the Tournament Program are countless! Here is a just a glimpse of the long list of Gun Clubs using Tournament for the administration of their competitions:

Arnold Rifle and Pistol Club
St. Louis, Missouri

Conventional Pistol, Highpower Rifle,
Action Pistol

 Bill Warren has been running outstanding Conventional Pistol matches here since the 1970's. The monthly regulars at the ARPC are some very familiar names in pistol circles: Jim Henderson, David Glenn, Paul Noles, Chris Guerrero, just to name a few. Bill has the distinction of being the first Match Director to ever use Tournament for an NRA Sanctioned competiton. Six years later, his competitors still receive their Tournament Match Bulletins by email.
Daniel Boone Gun Club
Evansville, Indiana

Conventional Pistol

Ken Odom, the Match Director of the Daniel Boone Conventional Pistol program is a proficient user of Tournament. Their annual NRA Regional Match at Evansville, Indiana is not only a very professional operation, but well-known among shooters all throughout the Midwest. Click HERE to see recent match results of their 2007 Regional as produced with Tournament.
Canton-McKinley R&P Club
Canton, Ohio

Conventional Pistol

 Stat Officer, Jeff Byham wisely made the decision to dump the NRA's antiquated DOS-based Tourney program soon after taking his position with Canton-McKinley. Hosting the Nation's most popular and attended Regional Conventional Pistol Match with over 250 competitors, Jeff has truly put Tournament to the test. Click HERE to see recent match results of the 2007 Regional Championship as produced with Tournament.
West Liberty Gun Club
West Liberty, Iowa

Conventional Pistol

 Greg Terry, the Conventional Pistol Match Director at West Liberty routinely hosts superb matches. With the help of Stat Officer JoAnn Page, West Liberty hosted the very successful 2007 Iowa State Championship welcoming competitors that traveled many miles from neighboring states. Click HERE to see the results of the State Championship as produced with Tournament.
Delaware State Pistol Club
New Castle, Delaware

Conventional Pistol

 Hank Maier does a fine job running the Conventional Pistol Program at the Delaware State Pistol Club. Taking on the challenge of hosting the Delaware State Indoor Championship required a solid scorekeeping software system. You can see the results of their State Championship as produced with Tournament by clicking HERE.
